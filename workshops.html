Workshops

These workshops document the cameraless animation and experimental film sessions hosted historically by Jacob Watkinson. Each session explored material animation, direct film techniques, and alternative image-making processes without a camera.

Workshop Topics

  • Direct animation on 16mm and Super 8 film
  • Photogram and chemigram-based imagery
  • Hand-drawn and painted animation on film leader
  • Experimental photographic motion through light and shadow
  • Collaborative material image-making and projection experiments

Past Sessions

Past workshops have been hosted in gallery and community spaces, focusing on playful, hands-on making while also introducing formal and conceptual processes from experimental cinema.

Approach

The workshops emphasise process over product. Participants are guided through a series of material experiments, then encouraged to develop personal imagery using both analogue and hybrid techniques.
