Void

2025

Synopsis

This work brings the perforation of 16mm film into focus, no longer a hidden mechanism, but as the image itself. Stripped of its traditional function as a mere apparatus for motion and projection, the perforation becomes a site of rhythm, texture, and flux. In the spirit of Structuralist film, the work reveals what cinema naturally conceals: the material body of the medium, its fragility laid bare.

The original synth score was composed and recorded by the Artist Henry Jackson-Newcombe to accompany the piece.
